Technical details

Pipeline. Sentence tokenization → MiniLM-L6 embeddings (L2-norm) → trajectory features (path length, variance, straightness, directional persistence, nearest-neighbor distances) → standardized logistic regression + probability calibration + tuned threshold.
Why it works. Human writing meanders with uneven pacing; LLM outputs are smoother. Geometry captures that without peeking at specific words.
Scope. Best on coherent prose (news, essays, reports). Use alongside human judgment for high-stakes decisions.
